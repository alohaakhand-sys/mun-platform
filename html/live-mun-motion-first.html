What the mockup is arguing

One gate, not four derivations

Both panes call one resolver over one floorGate field. Permission stops being derived, so flowModel, motionFloor, useFloorController and the projector can keep driving display without ever disagreeing about what is allowed.

The gate zone is the missing piece

Today the chair can never see what they have allowed. The middle zone is that answer, and it never collapses — unlike the current bar, which hides itself after six seconds.

Locked rows stay on screen

Most interfaces hide what you cannot do. In parliamentary procedure, knowing why you are out of order is the skill being taught. Every reason shown here is a real lockReason string from the shipped resolver.

Exactly one live action

Brass marks the open gate and the single action available. The delegate never has to choose between four buttons that lead to the same form — the problem the current console has.

Windows close themselves

Each gate carries endsAt. The client locks at the deadline and rules reject writes past it, so a sleeping chair laptop cannot leave a window open. No scheduled function, no Blaze.

Serif speaks, mono reports

Anything the committee says — the agenda topic, a motion, the country holding the floor — is set in serif. Anything the system knows — gate, timers, counts, status — is monospace.
