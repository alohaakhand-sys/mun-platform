Event Details, Committees, Partners

Event Details stacks its five cards: delegate information (expected delegates calculated from committees), web & social links, optional-module switches, marking templates (pattern, criteria with auto-fill and cap, starter set) and final scoring (chair overall weight, optional conference days). Committees keeps the "choices per applicant" stepper, template-or-scratch creation, seats, difficulty, agendas and the country roster picker. Partners can be reordered by dragging.
