AD02 · App Admin · Applications, Plans & revenue, Payments, Complaints

Approvals, money and escalations

Applications holds organizer requests, event upgrade requests and recent decisions. Plans & revenue totals Pro, ticket and fee income per event (developer-mode payments left out) and records refunds. Payments picks the gateway — Stripe, ZiniPay or Developer — shows which credentials are present, and settles organizer payouts. Complaints is the Level 3 queue with the full trail, reassign and final resolve / reject.
