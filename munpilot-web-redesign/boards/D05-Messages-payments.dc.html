Messages and Payments

Messages keeps the conversation list beside the chat, both dialogs (New Message, Create Group), presence, system notes and read ticks — and finally gets a phone layout: the list is its own screen, a chat opens on top. Payments keeps the three totals, the Transactions table with Pay Now or a printable receipt per row, the highlighted row and the success banner; on phones rows become cards. Sidebar abbreviated.
