Members, plans and approvals

Completes AD01 and AD02. The member page is drawn on desktop with the real Account copy, the verified tick, the "Disabled" badge, its messages and a proper delete dialog in place of today's prompt(). The plan dialog in request mode reviews a "Pro request" or "More room request"; in direct mode it gets the sixth feature, "QR coupons, check-in and transport scans", hides the limits when Pro is picked, and has no Cancel. Applications gets its real header, the upgrade-row details and both kinds of decision row. The event Payments dialog gets its exact copy and empty state.
