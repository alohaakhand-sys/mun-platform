Documents hub: every tab and role

Completes D02. Every role gets the Rules & Regs tab (file row, .md download, Read / Hide). Study Guide adds its PDF variant and empty state. Committee Docs adds the form errors, the roster warning, the country picker states, the Rejected pill, inline View / Hide, and the endorsement inbox with the exact Accept / Reject labels and the m:ss timer. Chairs and the EB get their four-tab review view. Observers and faculty get three read-only tabs, organizers a single "Manage" tab, and there's a fallback.
