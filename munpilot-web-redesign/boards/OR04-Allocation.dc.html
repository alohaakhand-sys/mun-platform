Allocation

One page, five tabs. Applications is a searchable table where Approve opens the applicant sheet (OR02) and unpaid rows can't be approved. Country Assignment counts down to the assignment deadline, flags past-event conflicts and saves per committee. Migration reviews delegate move requests (with the swap deadline) and lets the organizer move anyone directly. Capacity & Waitlist shows fill per committee and offers freed seats for 48 hours.
