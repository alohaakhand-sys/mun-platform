Modules, Complaints and Tools

Modules shows a tab per enabled module; each "Add" opens a dialog that starts from a quick template (Breakfast, Airport Shuttle, Twin Sharing, Business Formal…). Accommodation adds Pending Requests with Approve / Reject. Complaints escalated to you get Resolve, Escalate and Reject with an AI-assisted note. Tools: the hub, ID cards (template + who to print, one PDF), certificate templates (Ornate Gold is a Pro event feature), theme presets with a live preview, and Result Approvals with standings and the submitted awards.
