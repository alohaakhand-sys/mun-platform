Modules, ID cards and schedule

The Add Food Slot dialog gains "Who gets it" and "Uses per person", and the slot row now shows the audience. Add Transport Route gets its template grid and fields, plus a route card with a capacity bar. Dress Code gets its dialog, view and empty state. ID Cards adds "Print each person's QR pass on their card" (locked on Free) and the download states. The Schedule page gets its empty state and the "Add to Schedule" dialog with three ready-made templates and a blank session.
