Executive Board dashboard

Four counts on top (Committees, Delegates, Chairs, Graded %), then each conference in scope with its committees. A committee card shows delegates, chairs and whether a study guide exists; the ring and bar both show grading progress. The chair roster keeps the move-to-another-committee select (only when the scope covers several committees) and Remove, with the same confirmation. "Open Committee" goes to the hub.
