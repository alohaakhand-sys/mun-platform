Daily Marking Matrix and delegate record

Four tiles answer "how far along am I today". The toolbar keeps Apply suggestions, the day stepper, sort, Unscored only and search. The matrix keeps the sticky delegate column (flag, country, name, attendance, floor chips), one column per criterion with its scale, Daily % and the Committee mean row. Binary marks are a −1 / 0 / +1 segmented control; decimal marks a ±100 input; multi-mark cells open the record. The right-hand sheet keeps every section: day by day, the selected day's criteria, On the floor, how the final was computed, and private feedback. Right rail hidden at this width.
