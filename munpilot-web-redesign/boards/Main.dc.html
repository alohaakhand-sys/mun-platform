MUN Pilot web · foundations

Same layout, same colours — a calmer, sharper surface.

Every page keeps where things are today. What changes: one type system (Newsreader for display, Inter for the interface), softer 10–14px corners instead of 4px, quieter borders, real status colours, every table, form, empty and loading state designed — and a proper phone layout for every page. Live MUN is out of scope.

Loading

No spinners anywhere. The logo preloader for whole-app loads; skeletons everywhere else.
Preloader — munpilot-preloader.svg
The diamond dots the "i": it hops and turns once every 1.6s while "mun" and "pilot" breathe. Used only while the whole app boots — first visit, the dashboard's sign-in check, the app-return hand-off. Centered on the page background, nothing else on screen. Reduced-motion users get the still logo.
Skeletons — shimmer left to right, same shapes as the real content
Busy buttons don't spin: they say what's happening ("Saving…", "Sending…", "Redirecting to payment…") and stay disabled until done.

Page rules

Every page is drawn at 1440 and 390, and follows these.
Sidebar → drawerBelow 1024 the dashboard sidebar becomes a top bar with a menu button and a slide-in drawer. Same sections, same order.
Tables → row cardsOn phones each row becomes a card: name and status on top, two meta lines, actions in a ••• menu. No sideways scrolling.
Tabs scroll, actions stickTab rows scroll horizontally. A form's primary action sits in a sticky bar at the bottom of the screen.
Dialogs → sheetsCentered dialogs on desktop open as bottom sheets on phones, with the confirm button full width.
Glass sticky headerPublic header, dashboard top bar and portal top bar stay on top, frosted: paper at 78% with a 16px blur.
Grey and white bandsLong public pages alternate the grey page and full-width white bands, so the page never reads as one grey sheet.
Light by defaultNavy is kept for the hero and the organiser band. Search and inputs rest on a light fill with a hairline; card covers without a photo use sand.
Hover & focusClickable cards lift 4px with the overlay shadow and the title turns green. Focus is always a green ring, never just a colour change.
