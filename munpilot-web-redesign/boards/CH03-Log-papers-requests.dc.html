Motion Log, Papers, Requests

The Motion Log stays a read-only record for a chosen day: filter chips with counts, then motions, points, votes and yields in order, each with time, country, outcome and an expandable detail (a vote shows its tally bar and roll-call chips). Papers lists who submitted, with "Remind by email"; grading opens the two-pane viewer — paper on the left, status, score, feedback and private chair notes on the right. Requests is the Complaint Inbox with Resolve and Escalate to Organizer.
