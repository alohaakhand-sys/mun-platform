Awards & Results, Study Guide, Room Alert

Awards & Results reads in the order a chair works: publish the standings (full board or rank only, withdraw any time), compute and save final scores with the chair overall entered per delegate, then pick Best Delegate, Outstanding Delegate and Honorable Mention and submit them to the organizer. Study Guide is the Word-style editor with Write / Upload .md / Import PDF and Publish. Room Alert is a short form with a live preview of how it will look to delegates.
