Browse, payment states and receipts

The Browse tab (deep link ?tab=browse) gets its own grid of event cards with a placard, three meta rows and "View & Apply ›" or the disabled "Already Applied". My Conferences adds the overdue fee card, every status pill, due labels and its empty state. "Payment cancelled" (amber) and "Payment successful" (green) become one banner family that shows once and can be dismissed. Payments adds the Refunded row and receipt, plus inline Pay Now errors that replace today's alerts.

Conferences › Browse · with the cancelled-payment banner/dashboard/conferences?payment=cancelled — the banner sits between the header and the tabs; the param is removed so a refresh doesn't bring it back.
