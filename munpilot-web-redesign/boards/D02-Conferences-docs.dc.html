My Conferences and Documents

Conferences keeps My Conferences / Browse with counts, the role filter and sort, and Active & Pending above Past. Cards make the one thing that matters obvious: an unpaid card leads with the fee and "Pay", a pending card shows the Applied → Under Review → Decision tracker, an approved card goes straight to the portal. Documents keeps one card per conference with role-based sub-tabs; the delegate view is shown with the position paper under review and committee docs collecting endorsements. The sidebar here is abbreviated — the full one is on D01.
