Hub passes, read-only marking, edge states

The committee-hub rail adds "My Pass" and "Scan Passes" after Rules & Regs. Scan Passes needs the Scan Passes permission and opens the scanner. Gated tabs are left out of the rail, not locked; the "{label} is restricted" card appears only on a direct link. Staff get the same QR pass card as delegates. Viewers who can't mark see the matrix read-only, with no "Apply suggestions", faded binary cells and disabled inputs. The delegate detail sheet gets all its empty and edge lines.
